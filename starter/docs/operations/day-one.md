# Day one: the slow paperwork

Start these on day one, in parallel with Phase 0. They take days to weeks and none of them needs code.

Fees and waiting times change. The `operations` agent checks each on the provider's own page before you start, and records the date it checked.

## The company

| # | Item | Why now | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Form the LLC (or other entity) in your state | Everything below should be in the company's name | Ask a lawyer or an accountant about the state, the structure, and taxes. The assistant only drafts questions. |
| 2 | EIN from the IRS | Needed for the bank account and the store accounts | Free, online |
| 3 | Business bank account | Keeps the company's money apart from yours | Needs the EIN and the formation document |
| 4 | Operating agreement and a registered agent | Required or advisable, depending on the state | A commercial registered agent keeps your home address off public records |
| 5 | A mailing address that isn't your home | It goes in the terms, store listings, and the privacy policy | A virtual mailbox |
| 6 | D-U-N-S number for the company | Apple and Google both require it for an organization account | Free; can take days to weeks |

## Accounts, in the company's name from the start

| # | Item | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Domain | Turn on auto-renew and WHOIS privacy |
| 8 | Email forwarding: `support@`, `accounts@`, `legal@` | Sign up for every vendor with `accounts@`, so the accounts move with the company |
| 9 | GitHub repository | Private. Note whether your plan enforces branch protection |
| 10 | Apple Developer Program as an organization | Needs the D-U-N-S. Annual fee |
| 11 | Google Play developer account as an organization | One-time fee. New personal accounts must run a closed test with a minimum number of testers for a period before production; an organization account may avoid it, so check the current rule |
| 12 | Social handles on every platform you might use | Reserve them even if you won't post yet |

## Protection

| # | Item | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | Trademark clearance search, then an intent-to-use filing | In the company's name; after the name check in Phase 0 |
| 14 | Insurance: cyber liability, and technology errors and omissions | Before real users' data; essential at high risk |
| 15 | A lawyer for the compliance rounds (high risk) | Batch questions into rounds. The `legal` agent prepares each round. |

## Done when

Every row has a status and a date in `docs/operations/day-one.md` (copied from this file in Phase 0). Anything still pending says what it's waiting on.
